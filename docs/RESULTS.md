## thermd: deadlines under load

thermd samples both sensors every 10 ms at SCHED_FIFO priority; the deadline is the period. `hog` spins on every processor at priority 20. From the host, sensor 0x48 jumps to 91 C a third of the way through each run and back to 40 C at two thirds. Wake-up lateness and response time are measured from the start of each period, in microseconds.

| Run | thermd priority | Periods | Missed | No wake-up | Lateness p99 / max | Response p99 / max | Fan on after | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no load, interrupt mode | 30 | 6000 | 0 | 0 | 1151 / 2048 | 4068 / 6399 | 1434 µs | thermd-baseline.txt |
| hog at 20, interrupt mode | 30 | 6000 | 10 | 4 | 2488 / 23822 | 3702 / 33554 | 3705 µs | thermd-hog-irq.txt |
| hog at 20, polling mode | 30 | 6000 | 8 | 0 | 733 / 9601 | 4988 / 15585 | 4142 µs | thermd-hog-poll.txt |
| hog at 20, thermd below it | 10 | 6000 | 5800 | 5799 | 1184 / 57994388 | 3704 / 57995920 | never | thermd-hog-low.txt |
| no load in QNX, two busy loops on the host | 30 | 6000 | 2 | 0 | 634 / 4835 | 2580 / 14825 | never | thermd-host-load.txt |
| hog at 20, driver before the priority fix (20 s run) | 30 | 2000 | 1501 | 1499 | 1141 / 14991040 | 4620 / 15000971 | never | thermd-inversion-before-fix.txt |

What to read from this: below hog, thermd loses every period while hog runs and never sees the over-temperature; above it, it loses almost none. The few misses at priority 30 have wake-up lateness spikes of several milliseconds with nothing of higher priority running in QNX. The host-load run gets misses of the same kind with no load in QNX at all, only with the host busy, so they most likely come from the emulated processors not getting host time, which no priority inside the guest can fix. The host-load run has no temperature change if it was recorded before `measure.sh` included it.

## Recovery from a killed driver

`kill -9` of the driver every 2 s under `smb-supervisor`, with `thermd` running, from `recovery.txt`.

| Kills | Restarts | Restart time min / median / max | thermd gaps | Gap without data min / median / max | thermd missed deadlines |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 20 | 4 / 5 / 8 ms | 20 | 20 / 20 / 20 ms | 0 |

Restart time is from the supervisor's death notice (a `_PULSE_CODE_COIDDEATH` pulse) to `/dev/i2c0` open again. The gap is from thermd's last good sample before the kill to its first good one after, so it includes up to one 10 ms period of waiting.
